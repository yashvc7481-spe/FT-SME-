National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

The official Indian government portal to report cybercrime, online fraud, phishing attacks, social media crimes, and cyber bullying. Operated by the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.

When to contact Report here if you have experienced online fraud, phishing, hacking of accounts, ransomware, identity theft, financial cyber fraud, or any other internet-related crime. For financial fraud, also call 1930 immediately.

India's national nodal agency for cybersecurity incident response. CERT-In provides advisories on cybersecurity threats, handles vulnerability disclosures, coordinates incident response, and issues guidelines and alerts for the Indian cyber ecosystem.

Emergency Assistance

National Emergency Number — 112

India's single national emergency number for police, ambulance, and fire services. Operated by the Ministry of Home Affairs. Available 24×7 across all states and union territories.

When to contact Call 112 only for immediate physical emergencies — for example, if you are in immediate danger, have been threatened physically, or require urgent police, ambulance, or fire assistance. 112 is not a replacement for the dedicated cybercrime helpline (1930). For cybercrime and online fraud, use 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.

Financial Cyber Fraud

Reserve Bank of India (RBI) — Banking Fraud

India's central bank regulates banking and payment systems. The RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme covers complaints about banks, NBFCs, and payment system operators including UPI, digital wallets, and online banking fraud.

When to contact If you have experienced unauthorised banking transactions, UPI fraud, digital payment fraud, or your bank has not resolved your complaint within 30 days, escalate to the RBI Banking Ombudsman. Also contact your bank directly and the cybercrime portal (cybercrime.gov.in) at the same time.

Cybercrime Helpline vs. General Emergency — Know the Difference 1930 is India's dedicated cybercrime helpline — call it immediately for financial cyber fraud, phishing, or online crime.   112 is the general emergency number for police, ambulance, and fire services — use it only when there is immediate physical danger. Do not use 112 as a substitute for 1930 in cybercrime situations.

🛡️ What Should You Do During a Cyber Incident?

Follow these steps if you suspect or have confirmed a cyber incident. Acting promptly can limit damage and improve the chance of recovery.

1
Stop and do not click. Do not click suspicious links, open unexpected attachments, or download files from unverified sources.
2
Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if financial fraud has occurred. Request a transaction hold or block and raise a dispute as soon as possible.
3
For suspected financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 promptly and submit a report through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in).
4
Preserve all evidence — save transaction IDs, message screenshots, suspicious URLs, email headers, complaint reference numbers, and any other relevant information.
5
Report to the appropriate official authority — use the contacts above. For technical incidents involving organisational systems, report to CERT-In at incident@cert-in.org.in .
6
Change compromised passwords and secure affected accounts from a trusted, uncompromised device. Enable two-factor authentication where available.
7
Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, recovery codes, or private authentication keys with anyone claiming to provide support — including callers claiming to be from your bank, government, or a technology company.
